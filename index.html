Hybrid Training System

Workdays count as lower-body stress. The generated week schedules four required sessions and adds a fifth only when the week has enough recovery room.

Heavy Full-Body • about 60 min

  • Warm-up: bike/elliptical, knee-to-wall rocks, calf raises, ramp-up sets
  • Bench press 4×4–6
  • T-bar row 4×6–8
  • Leg press 3×6–10
  • Hamstring curl 2–3×8–12
  • Back extension 3×10–15
  • Seated calf raise 3×8–12
  • Banded ankle eversion 2×15/side

Keep about two reps in reserve on compounds and rest 2–3 minutes.

Arms & Shoulders • about 60 min

  • Seated dumbbell shoulder press 3×6–10
  • Cable lateral raise 3×12–20
  • EZ-bar or cable curl 3×8–12
  • Rope pushdown 3×8–12
  • Hammer curl 2×10–15
  • Overhead cable extension 2×10–15
  • Rear-delt fly 2×12–20

Return-to-Running + Ankle Armor • 30–45 min

Warm up with a brisk walk, ankle rocks, calf raises, and marching knee drives. Begin with the current run-walk stage and only progress after two successful sessions.

  • No running on valet days
  • No consecutive running days
  • Flat surface only; no hills, sprints, or hard intervals
  • One run each week is enough during the first block

Nickels & Dimes + Hybrid Conditioning • about 45 min

Keep this away from Heavy Day. It is automatically moved or removed when the workweek creates too much lower-body fatigue.

Raises & Reverses

This can be paired with Arms Day as a short finisher or used as the optional fifth session when the week is lighter.

Ankle Armor • 5–7 short sessions

  • Knee-to-wall rocks 10/side
  • Straight-knee calf stretch 30 sec/side
  • Bent-knee calf stretch 30 sec/side
  • Slow calf raises 12 with a 3-second lowering
  • Single-leg balance 30 sec/side

Minimum day: 10 ankle rocks and 10 calf raises.

Spacing rules: no Arms Day or Nickels & Dimes inside the 48 hours before Heavy Day. If work leaves you limping or unusually tight, replace the planned workout with mobility.

How the calculations work

Wake-up calculation

The planner prefers a 7:00 a.m. wake-up. Early work shifts move it earlier so the selected morning routine still fits before departure.

Bedtime calculation

Bedtime is based on the following morning's wake-up and your selected 7–8 hour sleep target. A warning appears when a late commitment makes fewer than seven hours likely.

GRCC commute

Current default: 20-minute drive + 5-minute rush addition + 15 minutes for parking and walking.

Work and gym

Current default: 18 minutes home to work plus a 10-minute arrival buffer. The gym is treated as 15 minutes from home and 10 minutes from GRCC or work.

Backup Rules & Data

If the manager schedule is difficult

  • Protect class and sleep first.
  • Keep Accounting's Tuesday same-day review whenever possible.
  • Reduce the optional fifth workout before removing a required session.
  • Move social time to the largest remaining opening instead of forcing a specific night.

If fatigue is high

  • Green: complete the workout as planned.
  • Yellow: remove one set or perform mobility only.
  • Red: do not run or train the painful area.
  • Never combine missed workouts to “make up” the week.

If schoolwork expands

  • Assignments due within 48 hours take priority over generic study blocks.
  • Shorten the optional workout before shortening sleep.
  • Use GRCC or a nearby coffee shop after class to avoid unnecessary driving.
  • Ask the professor for help within 48 hours of identifying a persistent problem.

After BA 103 ends Oct. 12

The generator automatically removes its recurring study target. Use the reclaimed hours for Accounting, Business Law, larger Computer Applications projects, recovery, or social time—not an automatic fourth workday.
